Community Guidelines & Terms
⚠️ Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
You must be 18 or older to use Bipolar Anonymous. Posting hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, or otherwise objectionable content — or abusing other members — will result in your content being removed and your access being permanently revoked.
The rules
💛  Be kind — everyone here is doing their best
🔒  Respect privacy — never share personal details, yours or others'
🚫  No objectionable content: hate, harassment, threats, spam, explicit material
🚨  Report anything that breaks these rules — every report is reviewed
🙈  Mute anyone you'd rather not hear from
Moderation

Reported content is reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. Objectionable posts are removed and the responsible member is ejected. You can remove your own posts at any time, and block any user from your settings.

Report a problem

To report inappropriate content or an abusive user, use the 🚨 button on any post, or contact us at bipolar@unisim.co.uk.

Bipolar Anonymous
A safe space for people living with bipolar

This is an anonymous peer community for people living with bipolar disorder. You join with a chosen name — your real identity is never stored or shared.

Community guidelines
⚠️  Zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users (18+)
💛  Be kind — everyone here is doing their best
🔒  Respect others' privacy — don't share personal details
🆘  Use the SOS flag if you're genuinely worried about someone
🚨  Report posts that break the rules — reviewed within 24 hours
🙈  Mute anyone you'd rather not hear from — their posts disappear for you
 ·  Report a problem: bipolar@unisim.co.uk
Post actions
💛  Like — show a post some love (you can't like your own)
💬  Comment — read replies and join the conversation (pulses when there are new replies)
🆘  SOS — quietly alert a moderator if you're worried about someone
🚨  Report — flag a post that breaks the rules — reviewed within 24 hours
🙈  Mute — hide everything from a user, just on your device
🗑️  Remove — delete one of your own posts (shown faintly on your posts)
Tap ⋯ on a post, or press and hold it, for Save, SOS, Report and Mute. Swipe a reply to the right to answer it.
