### About / Overview  (max 2000 chars)
```
Bipolar Bear makes two free apps for people living with bipolar disorder.

BIPOLAR BEAR — every high, every low
A private mood journal and mood tracker. Log your mood, energy and sleep in under a minute, see what shapes tomorrow's mood, and keep a survival kit of strategies, medications, goals and crisis resources for the hard days. Export a clean PDF summary for your doctor or psychiatrist. Journal entries are encrypted on your device before they leave it. Turn on Private journal and only you can read them, not even we can.
Web: https://bipolarbear.app
iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/app/bipolar-bear/id6766637453
Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bipolarbear.app

BIPOLAR ANONYMOUS — you're not alone
An anonymous peer community for adults living with bipolar. Share how you're doing, read how others are getting through, and offer support, all without sharing your identity. No names, no judgement. Whether you're newly diagnosed, supporting someone you love, or years into managing bipolar, you're welcome here.
Web: https://bipolaranonymous.app
iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/app/bipolar-anonymous/id6768005853
Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bipolaranonymous.app

Both are free on the App Store and Google Play, and on the web. No ads, and we never sell your data.

Bipolar Bear and Bipolar Anonymous are not medical devices and do not give medical advice. They are tools to help you notice, reflect and stay connected, alongside the care of your health professionals.

Made by James Markey MBE (jamesmarkey.co.uk) and the UNI·SIM team (unisim.co.uk).
```
