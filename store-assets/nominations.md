## 1. Bipolar Bear (`com.app.bipolarbear`, 6766637453)

- **Type:** App Enhancements
- **Name:** Bipolar Bear — a private mood journal for bipolar
- **Publish date:** from 2 November 2026 (after 1.41 is live)
- **Platforms:** iPhone, iPad · **Countries:** all · **Localisations:** automatic
- **Supplemental material:** https://www.bipolarbear.app/

**Description**

```
Bipolar Bear is a free mood journal for people living with bipolar disorder. Log mood, energy and sleep in under a minute with five mood bears (Manic, Elevated, Stable, Low, Depressed), see the patterns on a life chart, and keep a survival kit of coping strategies, medications and a safety plan for the hard days.

What's new this autumn:
• Privacy by design: personal details and the survival kit are now encrypted on the device before they reach the account, with the same key as the journal. A Private journal can be read by its owner alone.
• Mixed moods: hold a bear to add the other moods you felt that day.
• Bring your history across: import from Bipolar UK's Mood Tracker, or from any spreadsheet.
• Bear Arcade: short games about staying stable (keep the bear in the green zone, catch the things that keep you steady).
• The crisis button is in reach from the very first screen.
```

## 2. Bipolar Anonymous (`com.bipolaranonymous.app`, 6768005853)

- **Type:** App Enhancements
- **Name:** Bipolar Anonymous — peer support without names
- **Publish date:** from 2 November 2026 (after 1.40 is live)
- **Platforms:** iPhone, iPad · **Countries:** all · **Localisations:** automatic
- **Supplemental material:** https://www.bipolaranonymous.app/

**Description**

```
Bipolar Anonymous is a free peer-support board for people living with bipolar disorder. Members post under a moniker, never a real name, and get replies from people who know the highs and lows first-hand.

What's new this autumn:
• A safety net: when a post sounds like a crisis, the Friendly neighbourhood Bipolar Bear replies within seconds with crisis lines for the reader's own country and language, and a moderator is told so a person can check in.
• Read the board in your language: posts and the community wiki are machine-translated across 11 languages, with the original one tap away.
• More private: posts and replies no longer carry an account ID, and board profiles can't be looked up from an email address.
• "I needed this today" works without creating an account.
```
